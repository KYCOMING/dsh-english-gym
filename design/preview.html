AI Helps Small Teams Work Smarter

2026-11-23 · B1

AI 工具正在改变小团队的协作方式：把重复工作交给模型，人专注判断与沟通。

Small teams are using AI tools to take over repetitive work. Instead of spending hours on drafts and summaries, people now review what the model produces and spend more time on decisions.

The change is not about replacing people. It is about giving each person a little more room to think, while the routine tasks move faster in the background.
